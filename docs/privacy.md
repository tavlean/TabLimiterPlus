# Privacy Policy

Tab Limiter Plus runs locally in your browser. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

### What Tab Limiter Plus does

- Counts open tabs by total, per window, and per domain.
- Enforces your configured tab limits.
- Stores your tab limit settings in your browser's extension storage.
- Shows local notifications when a limit is reached.

### Data Tab Limiter Plus does not collect

- No personally identifiable information.
- No browsing history is stored.
- No analytics.
- No tracking.

Tab Limiter Plus does not contact any external server. Tab and domain information is used locally to count and enforce limits, and your settings stay in your browser's extension storage.

### Permissions

Tab Limiter Plus requests only the permissions it needs to count tabs, save settings, and show local notifications. Permissions are used solely for that purpose.

### Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the changes will be reflected here on this page.
